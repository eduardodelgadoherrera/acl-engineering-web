Services

Home automation

Lighting scenes, shades, climate, audio and access control from keypads and your phone. Designed by area and system, with integration of existing equipment.

Lutron lighting control

HomeWorks QSX, RadioRA 3 and Caséta: dimmers, keypads, processors and panels for architectural and landscape lighting (DMF, FX Luminaire). Programming backup and technical dossier included.

Motorized shades

Audio & video

Networks & security

Electrical work

Feeders, panels, outlets and accessories for residential and commercial projects, plus service for systems installed by others.

Do you work with US and Canadian homeowners?

Yes. Many of our clients are foreign homeowners and their builders or property managers. We communicate in English and Spanish, quote in USD and coordinate on site while you are away.

Yes. We diagnose, replace processors and modules, update programming and expand zones on existing Lutron, audio and network systems.

From plans or a site visit we prepare a proposal by area and system, with equipment, labor and clear scope. Quotes are valid for 10 days.
